# Creator money replies: five ready-to-paste macros

The five money questions creators ask most, written for Intercom. Each one says when to use it, what to check first, the reply, and what never to say. Every reply follows SideShift's Terms and never promises money a person hasn't approved.

Replace anything in `{curly braces}` before sending.

---

## 1. "Where's my withdrawal?" (still on its way)

**Use when:** the withdrawal is requested, awaiting payment or in transit, and today is on or before its expected arrival date.

**Check first:** the withdrawal's status, expected arrival date and trace code.

**Reply:**

> Hi {first name}, your withdrawal of {amount} is on its way. It's currently {status}, and the expected arrival date is {arrival date}. If your bank asks, the trace code is {trace code}. Banks can post it any time up to that date. If it hasn't arrived by the end of {arrival date}, reply here and I'll chase it with our payments team right away.

**Never say:** "It should be there soon." Always give the date.

---

## 2. "Where's my withdrawal?" (late)

**Use when:** the withdrawal is past its expected arrival date and still not completed.

**Check first:** the withdrawal ID, status, expected arrival date and trace code. Escalate to Payments with all four.

**Reply:**

> Hi {first name}, thanks for flagging this. Your withdrawal of {amount} should have arrived by {arrival date}, and it hasn't, so I've escalated it to our payments team with the transfer details. I'll update you here within one business day.

**Never say:** a new arrival date you haven't confirmed with Payments.

---

## 3. "Why can't I withdraw?" (identity check not done)

**Use when:** the creator has an available balance but hasn't completed identity verification.

**Check first:** verification status. If it shows *failed*, don't use this macro; send it to Trust & Safety.

**Reply:**

> Hi {first name}, your {amount} is ready to go. The only thing missing is a one-time identity check, which we need before any payout. Open Wallet, tap Verify identity and follow the steps (it takes a few minutes with your ID). Once it shows Verified, the Withdraw button will work.

**Never say:** anything about risk reviews or holds. If the account has a hold, this macro is the wrong one.

---

## 4. "I submitted my video and haven't been paid"

**Use when:** the submission is still waiting for the brand's review, and fewer than 5 calendar days have passed since it was submitted.

**Check first:** the submission date. Add 5 days to get the date the review window ends. If 5 or more days have passed with no decision, don't use this macro; escalate to Payments, because under the Terms the work counts as accepted.

**Reply:**

> Hi {first name}, your video was submitted on {submitted date} and the brand is still inside its 5-day review window, which ends {window end date}. Under our Terms, if they don't review it by then it counts as accepted, so you're covered either way. I'll keep this open and check on {window end date}.

**Never say:** "The brand will approve it." You don't know that, and the Terms already protect the creator.

---

## 5. "Why did less money reach our wallet?" (brands)

**Use when:** a brand funded its wallet and sees a lower credited amount.

**Check first:** the deposit's charged amount, processing fee and net credit.

**Reply:**

> Hi {first name}, here's the breakdown of your deposit on {date}: you were charged {charged amount}, {fee} of that was the payment processing fee for the funding method, and {credited amount} was credited to your wallet for creator pay. Processing fees aren't refundable under our Terms. If you plan to fund regularly, we can walk you through the lowest-fee way to do it for your plan.

**Never say:** "We'll refund the fee." Fees are non-refundable under the Terms, so any exception is a manager's decision.
